--- VERDICT (overturns the ZIL-condvar-only model) ---
OSV_ZIL_WAIT_TIMEOUT=1 (untimed cv_wait -> bounded cv_timedwait_hires, 1ms recheck of zcw_done)
STILL WEDGES. A 1ms-rechecking waiter with zcw_done==1 MUST proceed - unless the TIMER wake is ALSO
lost. Combined with qemu~50% (ONE vCPU busy, the rest halted and never rescheduled), this indicates a
SCHEDULER-WIDE cross-vCPU wake collapse: wakes to halted vCPUs (cv AND timer driven) are lost wholesale
under VU64 load. The ZIL commit-waiter (WALWrite, the global serialization point) is just where it first
manifests; zcw_done=1+asleep is the symptom, the disease is the OSv cross-vCPU wake-to-halted-vCPU path.
Refutations so far (ALL still wedge): FLUSH_INLINE, NO_MORPH, WAKE_IPI_ALWAYS (v1 and v2+mask-bypass),
ZIL_WAIT_TIMEOUT. => not the taskq hop, not morph/RHO, not the IPI-send guards, not the ZIL wait itself.
