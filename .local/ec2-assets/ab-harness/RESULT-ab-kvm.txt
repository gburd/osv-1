------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OSv boots cleanly under KVM (~430ms), imports+mounts the OpenZFS pgdata pool, the postmaster starts,
loads plpgsql, registers the "logical replication launcher" background worker -- then HANGS at its FIRST
fork() and NEVER reaches "ready to accept connections". Port 5432 never opens. Reproduced under BOTH
-accel kvm AND -accel tcg,thread=multi, at -smp 1 and -smp 8, with io_method=sync, autovacuum=off,
min workers, shared_buffers 128MB..1GB, with and without plpgsql preload. pg_controldata reads the data
dir fine (binary+datadir are compatible), so it is specifically the postmaster fork path.

This is the DOCUMENTED OSv fork-per-backend wall (ROADMAP lines 830-889, pg18-osv-result.txt):
OSv is a single-address-space unikernel; fork() gives the child a PRIVATE STACK but a SHARED heap /
globals / dynamic-linker state. Stock multi-process PostgreSQL forks a backend per connection expecting
COW-private address space; on OSv the first forked child hangs (shared-TLS/linker-lock, historically the
getenv() hang). This is a known-open OSv research problem (the reason a multithreaded PG19 port exists),
NOT a config or a KVM issue, and NOT fixable in this session. The fork-stack branch integ/pg-fork-zfs
@6dd041b advanced the fork mechanism (eager bind-now, fork-coherent PLT/GOT) but still does not carry
a multi-backend postmaster.

THE HONEST GAP (where OSv trails, mapped to the known lever):
  OSv trails by INFINITY at every cell (RW and RO, c1..c64): it cannot serve a single query, because the
  gating blocker is upstream of throughput -- it is CORRECTNESS of fork-per-backend, M1-gap item #2
  (the two fork faults) elevated to a total block on the multi-backend postmaster in single-address-space.
  The concurrency-ceiling / write-path / tuning levers (gap items #3/#4/#5) are all DOWNSTREAM and
  unmeasurable until PG serves. The single lever that matters here: a working private-per-child address
  space for fork (COW page-table contexts), OR the multithreaded PG port (apps/postgres, PG19). Until one
  of those lands, there is no OSv-vs-Linux PG number to compare -- the honest parity ratio is undefined.
