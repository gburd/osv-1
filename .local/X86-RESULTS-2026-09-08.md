## The bottleneck picture is FINER than the three-way split I banked
- **VU32**: unattributed stall. Device 15.7% util / 0.18 ms, `LWLock:WALWrite` only **1-5 of 32**,
  guest burning **1.6% of available CPU** with the host 99.8% idle. Work is neither on the device
  nor on a CPU.
- **VU96**: `LWLock:WALWrite` on **32-85 backends** with the device **93% idle** - i.e. the
  aarch64-style signature DOES appear on x86, but only at high concurrency.
So it is not "three arms, three answers": **the OSv WALWrite-on-idle-device signature is a
high-concurrency phenomenon**, and at moderate concurrency x86 shows a different, unattributed
stall. My earlier "CPU/guest-bound" label was retracted by the agent; `state='active'` in
`pg_stat_activity` means "not on a registered wait event", NOT "on CPU".

## Correction to a premise I had propagated to every agent
There is **no `zfs-2.4.1-osv` banner**. The guest prints `ZFS: OpenZFS 5000 initialized`, where
5000 is `SPA_VERSION_STRING` - the **pool format**, identical for bsd_zfs and OpenZFS. My
"prove OpenZFS at runtime via the banner" instruction was unfounded; the reliable proofs are
build-time (282 open_zfs objects vs 0 bsd `spa.c` objects; the four OpenZFS-only userspace libs).
