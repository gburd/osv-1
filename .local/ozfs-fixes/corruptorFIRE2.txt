Quarantine implemented VERBATIM per design in
  bsd/sys/cddl/compat/opensolaris/kern/opensolaris_kmem.c  (kmem_cache_free choke point):
  - N=4096 slot ring in static BSS (identity-mapped, never COW)
  - poison whole malloc_usable_size, record freeing backtrace, verify at eviction
  - layout-neutral, #ifndef WALL3_QUAR gate, CONF_fork
  Impl deltas forced by the platform (not design changes):
   * backtrace_safe is NOT dynamically exported to libsolaris.so (-z now) -> replaced
     with an inline frame-pointer walk (wq_backtrace).
   * kmem_cache_free runs in non-preemptable ZFS contexts -> ring bookkeeping under a
     spinlock, but poison/verify/free done UNLOCKED (page faults must stay preemptable);
     and skip quarantine when !sched::preemptable() (e.g. arc_buf_hdr freed under the arc
     hash_lock) since poisoning there would fault into an assert.

        (/usr/include/boost/intrusive/list.hpp: iterator_to: 1310)
      [backtrace]
        __assert_fail
        memory::page_range_allocator::alloc<true>(unsigned long, bool)+797
        memory::page_pool::l2::refill()+137
        memory::page_pool::l1::refill()+297
        memory::page_pool::l1::fill_thread()+332
      (run 1/2 showed the downstream form: EIO "could not read blocks ... in base/5/16399")

CONCLUSION — the corruptor is NOT a use-after-free / backward-overflow of ANY of the five
  ZFS metadata caches (abd_chunk, dbuf, arc_buf_hdr full/l2only, dbuf_dirty_record). Those
  are CLEAN. The wild write lands in the OSv PHYSICAL PAGE ALLOCATOR's free-list metadata:
  memory::page_range_allocator's boost::intrusive list of page_range free nodes is
  clobbered, so page_pool::l2::refill() walks a corrupted node and the intrusive-list
  invariant assert trips. i.e. something writes into a FREED/foreign page_range (or the
  page-pool free list) — a page-granularity wild write, not a kmem_cache object.

NEXT DIRECTION (narrowed): stop chasing ZFS kmem caches. Point the catcher at the page
  allocator instead:
   - guard/poison page_range free nodes in core/mempool.cc (page_range_allocator free path)
     — this is where the corruption is OBSERVED, so a quarantine/poison there will catch the
     writer at the moment it clobbers a free page_range (WALL3_FREE_GUARD in mempool.cc
     already validates pool page_header owner/cpu at pool::free — extend that to the
     page_range free list).
   - the abd_chunk cache uses PAGE_SIZE objects backed by whole pages; the ZFS write path's
     wild write is most likely into a PAGE it already returned to the page allocator (a
     page-level UAF), which is why an object-level kmem quarantine never sees it — the page
     is freed to the page_pool, re-handed to page_range_allocator, and THEN written.
