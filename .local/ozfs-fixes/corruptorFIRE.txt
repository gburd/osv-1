INTERPRETATION (consistent with prior 6-session notes /build/osv/.local/wall3.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The residual corruptor is NOT arena-VA-in-DMA. Combined with two prior findings:
  (a) the write bounce buffer is stable in-flight (prior abd_return_buf
      torn-in-flight check NEVER fired), and
  (b) no fork-arena VA is ever DMA-translated by an AS0/kernel thread (THIS run),
the wrong data is already present in the (coherent, identity-heap) SOURCE buffer
BEFORE the write is checksummed. The source is a DIVERGED / CLOBBERED IDENTITY
page, not an arena VA.

That is: the gather logic is fine, the alloc PLACEMENT is fine (already on the
identity heap), but the CONTENT of an identity-heap buffer is corrupted by a
neighbour -- an identity-heap OVERFLOW or USE-AFTER-FREE on the 8k-record ZFS
write path, exactly the prior notes' conclusion. It scales with the count of 8k
ZFS buffers (recordsize=32k survives) and write concurrency, and is masked by
large RAM (>=96G).

NEXT STEP (not this session's arena-DMA fix): targeted redzones on the hot ZFS
write-path kmem caches -- start with the abd_chunk cache and the dbuf cache
(highest churn on 8k records) -- a guard word per object checked on free, to
name the overflowing allocation at the moment of free with its freeing
backtrace. (prior notes recommendation #1). No arena-DMA fix is warranted; the
arena theory is dead.
