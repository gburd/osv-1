--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 READS (Linux control, quick observations -- full read is for the OSv A/B)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Scaling is healthy up to 64 clients on 48 node-0 cores. rw tps climbs
  monotonically 1->64 (412 -> ~12.2k); no cliff yet at 64 (< core count).
* Huge pages help most in the mid-range under contention:
  rw c=32 +15.4% (8272 -> 9543 tps), c=16 +4.5%; c=1/4 within noise (slightly
  lower, expected -- TLB win needs concurrency). rw c=64 flat (+0.3%), likely
  raidz/WAL-bound there, not TLB-bound.
  ro: hp=on modestly higher at every point (c=1 +6.0%, c=8 +6.9%) and notably
  TIGHTER variance (e.g. ro c=16 spread 3569 -> 1189).
* ro tps is ~20x rw at 64 clients (250k vs 12.2k) -- reads served from
  ARC/shared_buffers; writes pay raidz1+SLOG+WAL. This is the expected split
  and sets the ceiling the OSv virtio-blk path must approach.
* Variance is low relative to signal (rw spreads a few % of median); medians
  are trustworthy. Highest relative variance at rw c=32 (~16%), still the
  clear +HP win.
